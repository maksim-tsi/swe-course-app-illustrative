# Specialist role briefs

These files describe narrow AI assistance, not people. Each brief states when to use the profile, what it may propose, and the evidence it must return. The instructor is the sole operator and final decision maker for this demonstration.

- [Architect](architect.md): scope, alternatives and ADR reasoning.
- [Frontend developer](frontend-developer.md): user-visible states and accessibility observation.
- [Backend developer](backend-developer.md): domain rules, API and storage boundary.
- [Tester](tester.md): requirement-derived checks and observed limits.
- [Reviewer](reviewer.md): read-only diff and evidence critique.

Role briefs do not supersede [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), requirements, stage limits, or skills. W1 may use one or two roles only; do not simulate a large team for its own sake.
